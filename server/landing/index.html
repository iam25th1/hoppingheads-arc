6 WORLDS
Medieval villages, desert oases, frozen tundra, tropical islands, haunted graveyards, and crystal caverns. Each procedurally built.
♥
BOINK + ABSORB
Knock other players back with boink. Steal their fragments on hit. If you're bigger, fully absorb them and watch them shatter into cubes. Jump to dodge. Shield blocks hits.
♦
POWERUPS
Speed, Shield, and Magnet pickups spawn across the map. Speed doubles your movement. Shield blocks boinks. Magnet triples fragment detection range.
♠
MULTIPLAYER
Up to 8 players per lobby. Auto-matchmaking. Real-time positions, scores, and boinks. Connect your X account to play as @you.
♣
GROW + SHADOW
Every fragment makes your head bigger. Bigger means stronger boink range but slower movement. Go shadow form for 7 seconds of invisibility. Fever zones drop every 45s for 3x points.
♢
LEADERBOARD
Scores persist. Top players ranked by total score and wins. Your stats accumulate across rounds. Connect X to claim your spot.
THE LOOP
Collect. Fight.
Dominate.
01
COLLECT
Hop across the map finding rarity-tiered fragments. Common ones are everywhere. Legendaries are buried deep. Use Radar to reveal nearby hidden fragments.
02
FIGHT
Boink other players to steal fragments and deal damage. If you're bigger, fully absorb them. They shatter into cubes. Grab powerups. Use shadow form to go invisible for 7 seconds.
03
DOMINATE
Grow bigger than everyone. Absorb smaller heads to scatter their fragments. Grab the crown for +2 pts/sec while everyone hunts you. Use shadow form to escape or ambush. Open chests to heal.
